Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026. This policy covers NextLocal, an offline timetable for Mumbai's local trains and metro, published by Worldlamp Apps.

What we collect

Nothing. NextLocal has no user accounts and no sign-in. It does not collect, store or send personal information, and it has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.

Permissions

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

What stays on your device

Your app preferences, such as recent searches, language, time format and the "Holiday today" switch, are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Who the app talks to

The timetable is inside the app and works offline, so the app does not connect to any server of ours. If you choose to rate or share the app, or open a link, your device's app store, share menu or browser handles that, and their own privacy policies apply.

Timetable data

Timetables are compiled from published railway and metro timetables and other timetable data. NextLocal is not affiliated with or endorsed by Indian Railways, MMRDA or any metro operator.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: worldlampapps@gmail.com.
