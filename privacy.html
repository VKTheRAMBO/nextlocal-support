Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026. This policy covers NextLocal, a timetable for Mumbai's local trains and metro with live train delays shared anonymously by riders, published by Worldlamp Apps.

In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone. The only thing the app sends is a train's delay, anonymously, and only for a train you have confirmed you are on.

What the app does not do

NextLocal has no user accounts and no sign-in. It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. It does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone or files. The timetable is inside the app and works offline.

Location (optional)

When you first open the app, it explains live delays and your phone asks whether NextLocal may use your location while the app is open. If you allow it, the app compares your position with the timetable on your phone to suggest which train you may be on (you confirm it with a tap), to show how many minutes late your train is running stop by stop, and to offer the departures of the station you are at.

Your position is never stored on a server, never sent to us and never shared with anyone. Location is not used while the app is in the background. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

Shared train delays (optional)

When you confirm the train you are on, the app shares that train's delay so other riders can see it. Each report contains only: the train (its timetable id) and the date it is running; how many minutes late it is; the last station it has passed; and a random number made for that one trip, so the same trip is not counted twice.

A report never contains your location, name, phone number, device id, advertising id or any account. Reports go to our delay server, hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps them for 20 minutes to show them to other riders and then deletes them. Like any internet service, the server sees your IP address when a report arrives; it is used only, in memory, to stop floods of reports and is discarded within a minute. It is never logged, stored or linked to reports.

To stop sharing, turn off location for NextLocal in your phone's settings: without location the app never suggests or follows a train, so it has nothing to share. The app still downloads other riders' delays (a plain request that sends nothing about you) so you can see which trains are late.

What stays on your device

Your app preferences, such as recent searches, language, time format and the "Holiday today" switch, are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Other services

If you choose to rate or share the app, or open a link, your device's app store, share menu or browser handles that, and their own privacy policies apply.

Children

NextLocal is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Independent app

NextLocal is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Indian Railways, MMRDA or any metro operator. Times, platforms, fares and delays are indicative estimates; always check station indicators and announcements.
