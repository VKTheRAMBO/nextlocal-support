Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026. This policy covers NextLocal, an offline timetable for Mumbai's local trains and metro, published by Worldlamp Apps.

What we collect

Nothing. NextLocal has no user accounts and no sign-in. It does not collect, store or send personal information, and it has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.

Permissions

The app does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Location (optional). Only if you tap "Track this train" does the app ask for your location, and only while the app is open. It compares your position with that train's timetable on your phone to show how late the train is running. Your location is never stored, never sent to us and never shared with anyone. Tracking stops when you tap Stop, when you arrive or when you close the app. You can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

What stays on your device

Your app preferences, such as recent searches, language, time format and the "Holiday today" switch, are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Who the app talks to

The timetable is inside the app and works offline, so the app does not connect to any server of ours. If you choose to rate or share the app, or open a link, your device's app store, share menu or browser handles that, and their own privacy policies apply.

Independent app

NextLocal is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Indian Railways, MMRDA or any metro operator. Times, platforms, fares and delays are indicative; always check station indicators and announcements.

Changes and questions
